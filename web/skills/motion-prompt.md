# Motion & Video Prompts

Structure every shot prompt as:
1. **Subject & action**: who/what, doing exactly what, with one clear motion beat.
2. **Camera**: shot size (wide, medium, close-up, macro), movement (static, slow dolly-in, orbit, crane up, handheld), lens feel (24mm wide, 85mm portrait), speed.
3. **Setting & light**: place, time of day, light quality and direction (soft window light, hard rim light, neon practicals).
4. **Style**: medium (photoreal, 3D render, claymation, anime, product commercial), colour palette, film stock or grade.
5. **Timing**: duration, pacing, where the beat lands; for sequences, list shots with timestamps and transitions (match cut, whip pan, morph).
6. **Negative / constraints**: no text artifacts, no extra limbs, keep logo shape consistent.

Keep one idea per shot, concrete nouns and verbs, and avoid stacking contradictory styles. When a reference video is given, describe its camera path, rhythm and colour first, then adapt.
