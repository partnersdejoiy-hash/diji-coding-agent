# Redesign Existing UI

1. Inventory what must survive: every piece of content, feature, link, form field and behaviour. Nothing gets silently dropped.
2. Diagnose in one list: what is the primary action, what competes with it, where spacing is uneven, which colours have no job, what fails contrast or breaks on mobile.
3. Decide: refinement (keep the identity, fix craft) or redesign (new visual world, same product truth). Do not half-polish a look you are replacing.
4. Rebuild with tokens (colour, type scale, spacing, radius) defined once, then components.
5. Show the change: summarise before → after in 3–5 bullets (hierarchy, spacing, colour, responsiveness, accessibility).
Keep real copy unless asked to change it; flag any copy you think is weak instead of rewriting it quietly.
