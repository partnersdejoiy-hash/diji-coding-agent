# Diji app shell (chat, workspace, settings)

Scope: the whole Diji web app, `web/index.html` and every screen it renders. Mode: Operate.
Audience and job: everyone coding with Dejoiy; ask, build, preview, fix, ship. Long sessions, day and night, laptop and phone.
Constraints: keep all behaviour and copy; light and dark both first-class; no blue/purple AI gradient look; Dejoiy pink and blue stay the brand inks.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Diji looks printed, not rendered: a two-ink risograph world on paper, applied to a standard chat-plus-workspace app. It refuses the category default of navy glass, glow and blue-violet gradients.

OWN-WORLD: Newsprint paper ground (light: warm off-white; dark: plum-black paper), a pink-tinted sidebar rail. Two inks only: Dejoiy magenta (actions, brand, the mascot) and Dejoiy blue (links, selection, focus, code). Near-black warm ink for text. Flat surfaces with 1px ink rules, small radii, a faint paper grain on the ground. Type: Bricolage Grotesque for display and headings, Schibsted Grotesk for UI and reading, JetBrains Mono for code.

STORY: People see a calm, familiar chat app that feels hand-made; they type, Diji (a pink mochi with a sprout) squishes while working, files appear as cards, the preview opens beside the chat.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left: pink-paper rail with wordmark, New chat, search, chats, Skills/Settings at the foot. Centre: mochi mascot, a large Bricolage greeting with a misregistered pink shadow, two-by-two starters, the composer directly under them with a solid magenta send button. Top right: Workspace and Browser buttons.

FORM: two-ink risograph print, candidate 1 of 7 on the ordered list (user's choice over the assigned candidate 5, bubblegum studio). Signature move: misregistered overprint, a blue tint block with a pink duplicate offset a few pixels, marks every selected or active thing (current chat, selected tab, chosen model, user bubbles, the greeting). seed:7053fe19de1a3faa

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
